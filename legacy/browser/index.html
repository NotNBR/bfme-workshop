COMMANDER’S FIELD MANUAL

Your army. Your orders.

Select
Left click a battalion, or drag a selection box. Shift adds to selection. Double click selects visible battalions of that type.
Command
Right click to move. A then click / right click to attack-move. Shift queues waypoints. H holds ground, S stops.
Strategic camera
Mouse wheel zooms around the cursor. Middle drag or arrow keys pan. Tab toggles whole-map view. Click minimap to jump. F focuses selection.
Army tools
Ctrl + 1–9 saves a group; 1–9 recalls it. F2 selects the army. Choose formations in the command bar.
Economy & powers
Hold an uncontested outpost for 8 seconds to gain +10 resources/s. Recruit using the cards. Click a queued unit to cancel for 75% refund. Q then click heals an area for 30 power.
Battle
Space pauses. Reinforcements attack-move to your rally point. Destroy the enemy fortress to win. Terrain is currently traversable; fog of war and multiplayer are not implemented.
